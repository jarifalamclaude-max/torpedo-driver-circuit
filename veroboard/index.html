Before you solder

  • This is drawn for dot-type veroboard (one pad per hole, 2.54 mm). Every line is an insulated wire on the solder side, so wires may cross. On strip-type board you would have to cut the strips under every part first.
  • Thick lines carry battery and solenoid current (about 1.1 A per 15 Ω solenoid). Use 0.5 mm² (20 AWG) or thicker for them. Thin lines are signals; 30 AWG wire-wrap or thin hookup wire is enough.
  • The XT60 board connector does not fit a 2.54 mm grid. Solder an XT60 pigtail or a screw terminal at J1.
  • TO-220 pins are G, D, S left to right with the printed face toward you. PC817 pin 1 is at the dot. Check 1N5819, zener and TVS bands against the K mark.
  • R12 (15 k) across the arm switch is the self-test pull-up from your design, so VSW is not at 0 V when disarmed.
