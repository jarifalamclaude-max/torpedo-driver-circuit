Dui rokom MOSFET kn?

MOSFET on hoy gate ar source-er parthokko diye, gate-er nijer voltage diye na. N-channel on hote gate-ke source-er upore ~10 V nite hoy. P-channel on hote gate-ke source-er niche ~10 V namate hoy. Tai kon dike bosche tar upor type thik hoy.

KothaySource koto V-eN-channel dile gate lagbeP-channel dile gate lagbeJa nisi
− dik (Q1, Q2)0 V (GND)0 + 12 = 12 V. Board-e ache0 − 10 = −10 V. Board-e negative voltage naiN-channel IRLZ44N
+ dik (Q3)16.8 V (battery)16.8 + 10 = 26.8 V. Board-e eto voltage nai, alada driver IC lagto16.8 − 11.4 = 5.4 V. Shudhu GND-er dike tanlei hoyP-channel IRF4905

N-channel eki dame kom resistance dey, tai jekhane shombhob (− dik) N. + dike P nile ekta optocoupler ar duita resistor diyei kaj hoy.

Kaj

F1RUEF300 PTC fuse · 3 A hold, 6 A trip, 30 V
Kaj
Board-e short hole garom hoye current komiye dey. Thanda hole nije thik hoy, bodlate hoy na.
Value
I_max = 2 × VBAT / R_sol = 2 × 16.8 / 15 = 2.24 A (dui solenoid ekshathe). Eta hold current 3 A-er niche thaka chai.
Kom hole
1.85 A hold nile dui solenoid ekshathe dile trip korte pare. Garom hull-e hold current aro kome.
Beshi hole
5 A hold nile short-e trip korte deri, 1.2 mm track (≈ 2.7 A) age garom hoy.
Na thakle
Short hole LiPo shoto ampere dey: track pure, agun-er bhoy.
Mone rekho
PTC dhire kaj kore (second-er hisab) ar 100 A porjonto shoy. Battery-r dead short-er jonno main fuse alada lagbe.
C1470 µF 35 V electrolytic
Kaj
Current hothat bodlale battery tar-er inductance voltage laf dey. C1 sheta sokhe.
Value
ΔV = I × √(L_tar / C). 1 m tar ≈ 1 µH. C1 thakle 1.11 × √(1µ / 470µ) = 0.05 V. Na thakle (shudhu ~1 nF) 1.11 × √(1µ / 1n) = 35 V.
Voltage
35 V ≥ 2 × 16.8 V. TVS-er 27.7 V clamp-eo thake.
Kom hole
10 µF: ΔV = 0.35 V. Chole, kintu ADC reading kape.
Beshi hole
4700 µF: battery laganor shomoy boro spark, jayga beshi.
Polarity
Lomba pa +. Ulta lagale fete jay.
D4P6KE20A TVS diode
Kaj

Value
Duita shorto: V_standoff 17.1 V ≥ 16.8 V (full battery-te chup thake) ar V_clamp 27.7 V < 35 V (C1, optocoupler-er limit).
Kom hole
P6KE15A (12.8 V): full battery-tei current tane, garom hoye pure.
Beshi hole
P6KE33A (clamp 45.7 V): C1 ar optocoupler-er 35 V limit par hoye jay.
Na thakle
Lomba tar ba thruster-er spike sorasori MOSFET ar C1-e pore.

Kaj

Zener kn
IRLZ44N-er gate 16 V-er beshi shoy na. Battery 16.8 V, spike-e 27.7 V.

Low battery

10 kΩ: 13.2 × 4.7 / 14.7 = 4.2 V, gate durbol, MOSFET garom.
R9 chhoto hole

C4
Rail-er noise komay. Gate on korte lage ~50 nC, C4-e ache 100 nF × 12 V = 1200 nC.

Flyback diode ar LED

D1 · D21N5819 Schottky · 40 V, 1 A
Kaj
Coil current hothat thamte pare na. MOSFET bondho hole ei diode current-ke coil-ei ghurar rasta dey.
Value
Joma shokti E = ½ × L × I² = ½ × 0.03 × 1.11² = 18 mJ. Diode thakle drain peak VBAT + V_f = 16.8 + 0.45 = 17.2 V. Shesh hote 3 × L / R = 3 × 30 mH / 15 Ω = 6 ms.
Rating
40 V > 27.7 V clamp. 1 A gor, 25 A surge; ekhane 1.1 A matro 6 ms.
Na thakle
Voltage MOSFET-er 55 V limit porjonto uthe, protibar 18 mJ MOSFET-er upor pore. Dhire dhire nosto hoye short hoy.
Ulta lagale
Fire korlei diode sorasori short. Dag (band) + dike thakbe.

Voltage mapa ar self-test

R5 · R6 · C3
R10 · R11 · C522 kΩ · 2.2 kΩ · 100 nF
Kaj
Battery (VBAT) ar switch-er porer rail (VSW) ESP-er jonno chhoto kore. R5/R6/C3 → GPIO3, R10/R11/C5 → GPIO4.
Value
V_ADC = V × R6 / (R5 + R6) = V / 11. 16.8 V → 1.53 V, 13.2 V → 1.20 V. ESP32-C3 bhalo mape ~2.5 V porjonto. Spike 27.7 V → 2.5 V, tao nirapod.
Ratio kom hole
÷ 4: 16.8 V → 4.2 V, ESP pin nosto.
Ratio beshi hole
÷ 50: 0.34 V, reading mota hoye jay.
Ohm boro hole
220k / 22k: current kom, kintu moisture leak-e reading beshi nore.
Ohm chhoto hole
2.2k / 220 Ω: 7 mA shob shomoy khoroch. Ekhon 16.8 / 24.2k = 0.69 mA.
C3, C5
τ = (R5 ∥ R6) × C = 2 kΩ × 100 nF = 0.2 ms, noise filter. 10 µF dile τ = 20 ms, firmware-er 4 ms opekkhay reading thik ashbe na.
Na thakle
Battery voltage jana jeto na: pulse limit, self-test, NO_POWER kichui hoto na.
R1215 kΩ self-test resistor
Kaj

Value

Test current
VBAT / R12 = 16.8 / 15k = 1.1 mA. Solenoid norte shoto mA lage.
Chhoto hole
150 Ω: Q1 short hole solenoid-e 100 mA, R12-e 1.6 W.
Boro hole
1 MΩ: r = 0.02, "thik" ar "Q1 short" alada kora jay na.
Na thakle
Q3 off-e VSW = 0, tai fire MOSFET short holeo bujha jeto na.
