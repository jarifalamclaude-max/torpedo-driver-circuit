Isolation: GNDL ar GNDP

GNDL · GNDPduita alada ground plane
Kaj
ESP32 (GNDL) ar battery (GNDP)-r majhe kono tamar jog nai. Board-er majhe 6.2 mm chowra channel-e kono copper nei; shudhu chhoyta PC817 oi channel-er upor bose.
Kn
v3-e ESP32-er GND ar battery − ek chhilo, tai "opto isolation" shudhu nam-e chhilo. Ekhon MOSFET fete gele ba solenoid-er tar short hole battery voltage ESP ar Jetson-e pouchay na.
Sim
GNDL-ke 1 GΩ ∥ 10 pF diye GNDP-r shathe dhore, fire-er shomoy isolation diye current 1 µA-r kom.
Shorto
Logic 5 V (J4 ba USB) ar battery − kothao ek kora jabe na. Jetson jodi eki battery-te chole, tobe ESP-er 5 V isolated DC-DC theke dao.

Kaj

Ulta
GPIO HIGH mane line LOW (LED jole, transistor tane). Firmware eta mathay rekhe bit-bang kore.
Speed
Opto dhire: sim-e shob kharap corner-e (CTR 50 %, 75 pF) shobcheye dhir edge 142 µs. Tai half-bit 500 µs, ~1 kHz I²C. Ekta reading-e kichu ms lage, eta jothesto.
R18 1 kΩ
U7 LED ~1.9 mA. ADS-ke mot 2.5 mA sink korte hoy, ADS1115 3 mA-e 0.4 V porjonto pare; sim-e SDA low 0.25 V.

R20 · R21
